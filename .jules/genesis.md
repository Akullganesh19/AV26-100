## 2026-06-21 — Protected Third-Party API Integrations
**Failure point found:** External API integrations (Algolia, SendGrid, Cloudinary) in `backend/app/api/integrations.py` were making raw calls without any retry or circuit breaker mechanisms. A transient failure in SendGrid or Algolia would hard-fail the asynchronous workflow, potentially leaving the database out of sync with the search index or failing to deliver critical alerts.
**Why it existed:** The initial implementation correctly offloaded blocking I/O to threads (`asyncio.to_thread`) but stopped there, assuming perfect network reliability and constant availability of third-party systems. Furthermore, the `upload_report_to_cloudinary` function missed the `asyncio.to_thread` wrapping, exposing the event loop to blocking I/O.
**Recovery built:** Created `backend/app/core/resilience.py` introducing `@with_retry`, `@with_circuit_breaker`, and `@with_dead_letter_queue` decorators. Wrapped vulnerable network calls in `backend/app/api/integrations.py` with exponential backoff retries and circuit breakers. Wrapped Cloudinary upload in `asyncio.to_thread`.
**Blast radius before:** A brief outage or rate-limiting of a third-party service would result in permanent mission failure for the specific request, such as a dropped critical health alert.
**Watch for:** Other unpatched asynchronous background tasks or third-party webhooks that lack idempotency or retry protections.
