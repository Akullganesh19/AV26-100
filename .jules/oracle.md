## 2024-05-18 — Behavioral & Intent Prefetching

**Product understood as:** An epidemiological command center for real-time risk monitoring, mission simulations, and clinical diagnoses across districts.
**Prediction invented:** 1. Silent pre-generation of PDF tactical reports immediately after a clinical diagnosis is made. 2. Intent-based prefetching of specific district data on the Strategic Map immediately when a user hovers over the tooltip.
**Data used:** 1. Diagnosis result state transition (Behavior). 2. Map mouseover events (Intent).
**Impact:** 1. Zero-latency instant downloads for mission reports instead of waiting for the backend to generate them on click. 2. Zero-latency drilling down into a district's diagnostics view from the map.
**Next opportunity:** Prefetch simulation scenario details when a user navigates to the Simulation Lab before they even launch a specific mission.
