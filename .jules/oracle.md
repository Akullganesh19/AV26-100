## 2026-06-16 — Predictive Intent Route Prefetching
**Product understood as:** A predictive epidemiological monitoring platform used by health officers to forecast disease outbreaks and simulate scenarios.
**Prediction invented:** Predictive Route Data Prefetching. Detects when a user moves toward a navigation link (hover) and instantly prefetches the required data (dashboard stats, map districts, tactical alerts, or scenarios) before they click.
**Data used:** User's cursor movement intent (onMouseEnter event on primary navigation links).
**Impact:** Reduces perceived load time from ~400ms to near zero when navigating between core views, making the app feel impossibly fast and ahead of the user.
**Next opportunity:** Pre-fill clinical triage forms based on the disease context of the alert the user just clicked.
