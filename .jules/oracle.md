## 2024-08-26 — Hover-Based Predictive Data Prefetching
**Product understood as:** A real-time epidemiological monitoring and predictive risk analysis platform.
**Prediction invented:** Anticipating user clicks on the tactical map to prefetch district details for the clinical triage center.
**Data used:** User hover/mouseover events on the map districts.
**Impact:** Eliminates data loading latency when navigating to the clinical triage center, providing a near-instant experience.
**Next opportunity:** Predicting the most likely disease screening (heart, diabetes, parkinsons) based on district demographics and pre-filling forms.
