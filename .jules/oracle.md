## 2024-10-24 — Intelligent Defaults and Behavioral Prefetching
**Product understood as:** An epidemiological intelligence platform designed for regional health mission coordination.
**Prediction invented:** Intelligent defaults for Clinical Diagnostics and background behavioral prefetching for Strategic Maps.
**Data used:** For Intelligent Defaults: historical user audit logs (`PredictionAuditLog`) predicting next screening disease and district. For Behavioral Prefetching: mouseover events on map features predicting user drill-down intents.
**Impact:** Eliminates repetitive clicks during clinical screening flows by automatically populating the most likely disease form and district. Eliminates perceived latency when a user navigates to district details from the strategic map by pre-loading the data silently on hover.
**Next opportunity:** Session warm-up based on a user's role and time of day, prefetching the most active threats directly on login.
