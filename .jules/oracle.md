## 2026-06-16 — Predictive Tactical Report Generation
**Product understood as:** A system to diagnose and assess risks for diseases (Heart Disease, Diabetes, Parkinson's) and provide tactical insights.
**Prediction invented:** Anticipating that a user will download the tactical report after receiving a diagnosis, we pre-generate the PDF report in the background immediately upon diagnosis completion.
**Data used:** The generated diagnosis/prediction results right after a user runs the clinical diagnosis form.
**Impact:** Eliminates the 2-5 second wait time to generate the report when the "Tactical Report" button is clicked. The PDF is downloaded instantly.
**Next opportunity:** Prefetch patient/district metadata intelligently based on recent session history to pre-fill the form inputs.
