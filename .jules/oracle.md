## 2025-02-15 — Tactical Report Prefetch
**Product understood as:** A clinical screening platform for early detection of diseases to support tactical alerts.
**Prediction invented:** Behavioral Prefetching of Tactical Reports for high-risk patients. When a user receives a high-risk diagnosis, they inevitably need the tactical report for follow-up actions. Instead of waiting for them to request it, the app instantly pre-generates the report in the background.
**Data used:** The clinical screening result signal (risk = true) triggers the prediction.
**Impact:** Users click 'Download Tactical Report' and it resolves instantly (0ms perceived latency), feeling remarkably responsive and predictive of their immediate next requirement.
**Next opportunity:** Predicting navigation flow. Prefetching data for the Strategic Map and Tactical Alerts sections when a new cluster is detected.
