## 2026-07-23 — Predictive Intent & Triage Engine
**Product understood as:** An epidemiological command center used by health officers to monitor disease outbreaks, receive early warnings, and simulate/investigate risk.
**Prediction invented:**
1. Route Prefetching: Anticipates navigation intent by prefetching data when hovering over sidebar links.
2. Triage Anticipation: Predicts that a user hovering over a high-risk district on the map will investigate it, prefetching its deep details.
3. Post-Action Prediction: Predicts a user will view the strategic map after acknowledging an alert to see the updated global status, prefetching map data in the background.
**Data used:** User cursor position (hovering links and map features) and completed tactical actions (acknowledging an alert).
**Impact:** Zero perceived latency when navigating to key dashboards, and instant data availability when drilling down into critical risk sectors.
**Next opportunity:** Predicting which parameters a health officer is likely to tweak in the Scenario Lab based on recent environmental trends.
