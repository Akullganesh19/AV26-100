## 2026-06-16 — Intelligent Route Prefetching for Map
**Product understood as:** Epidemiological monitoring and predictive risk analysis platform.
**Prediction invented:** Intelligent route prefetching on the Strategic Map. When a user hovers over a district, we predict they will likely investigate it next, so we prefetch the detailed district data in the background.
**Data used:** User behavior signal (cursor hover position on the Leaflet map).
**Impact:** Eliminates perceived load time when a user navigates to the Diagnostics Center or district detail view.
**Next opportunity:** Prefetching specific disease risk timelines based on active map filters.
