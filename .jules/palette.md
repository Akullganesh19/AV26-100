## $(date +%Y-%m-%d) — Form Accessibility and ARIA Overhaul
**Found:** Several critical forms (`DiagnosticsCenter.tsx`) lacked proper `id` to `htmlFor` bindings between inputs and labels. Additionally, numerous icon-only buttons across the app (like sidebar toggles and password visibility) lacked `aria-label`s, and asynchronous submit buttons were missing `aria-busy` and `aria-hidden` on nested loading icons.
**Why it existed:** Likely developed quickly focusing on visual layout and state management over accessibility standards. `React.useId()` usage wasn't standard practice for dynamically rendered inputs.
**Fix:** Refactored the `Input` and `Select` helper components to automatically generate unique IDs using `React.useId()` and bind them to `<label>` elements. Added descriptive `aria-label` attributes to icon-only buttons in `MainLayout.tsx` and `LoginPage.tsx`. Added `aria-busy={loading}` to asynchronous submit buttons and `aria-hidden="true"` to decorative icons within them.
**Learning:** When building or modifying React form helpers, `React.useId()` is essential for robust accessibility without requiring manual prop passing for IDs. Ensure all state-driven buttons explicitly communicate their active state to screen readers.
**Watch for:** Future additions of standalone `input` fields or icon buttons that skip these foundational accessibility attributes.
